How accurate are race time predictions?

Riegel's formula works well between 5K and half marathon if you have trained for the longer distance. For the marathon it is usually optimistic: most recreational runners finish 5 to 10 percent slower than the prediction unless they have run 50+ km a week and long runs of 30 km or more.

  • Use a recent result. A race from the last 6 to 8 weeks, run flat out on a flat course.
  • Predict from the closest distance. A half marathon predicts a marathon far better than a 5K does.
  • Treat the marathon number as a ceiling. Start at the predicted pace plus 5 to 10 seconds per km and speed up after 30 km if you feel good.

Common questions

What marathon time does a 25 minute 5K predict?

Riegel's formula predicts about 3:59:47 for the marathon and 1:55:00 for the half. Most runners without high mileage should expect a marathon 5 to 10 percent slower.

What half marathon time does a 50 minute 10K predict?

About 1:50:19, which is 5:14 per kilometre.

What is Riegel's formula?

It is a race time prediction published by Peter Riegel in 1977: T2 = T1 × (D2 / D1)^1.06. The exponent 1.06 reflects how much a runner slows down as the distance increases.

Can I run a sub-4 marathon?

A 4:00:00 marathon roughly matches a 25:01 5K or a 1:55:07 half marathon by Riegel's formula, but you also need the endurance from several months of consistent training.
