Common questions

What is a good 5K time?

For most recreational runners, finishing under 30 minutes is a solid goal, which is about 6:00 per km or 9:39 per mile. Under 25 minutes (5:00/km) puts you well ahead of the average parkrun field, and sub-20 (4:00/km) is a strong club-level time.

How do I run a faster 5K?

Combine one interval session a week (for example 5 × 1 km at goal pace with short recoveries) with mostly easy running. Most improvement for beginners comes from running more often at an easy, conversational pace.

What pace is a 25 minute 5K?

A 25:00 5K is 5:00 per kilometre or 8:03 per mile.
