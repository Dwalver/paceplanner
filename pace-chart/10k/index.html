Common questions

What is a good 10K time?

Under one hour is the classic first goal for a 10K, which means 6:00 per km or 9:39 per mile. Sub-50 (5:00/km) is a strong recreational time and sub-40 (4:00/km) is competitive at most local races.

What pace is a 50 minute 10K?

A 50:00 10K is 5:00 per kilometre or 8:03 per mile, with a 25:00 split at 5 km.

Should I run even splits in a 10K?

Yes. Aim to pass 5 km within a few seconds of half your goal time. Starting too fast is the most common reason runners miss their target.
