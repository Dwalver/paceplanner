FAQ

How do I calculate my running pace?

Divide your finish time by the distance. 50:00 for 10 km is 5:00 per km, or about 8:03 per mile.

How accurate is the race predictor?

It uses the Riegel formula, T2 = T1 × (D2 / D1)1.06. It is usually within a few percent for nearby distances. Marathon predictions from short races assume you have done the long-run training, so treat them as a best case.

How fast should my easy runs be?

Roughly 25–38% slower per km than your current 5K race pace. If you can't hold a conversation, slow down. Most runners run their easy days too fast.

What is threshold or tempo pace?

A comfortably hard pace you could hold for about an hour in a race. Training there raises the speed you can sustain before fatigue builds up quickly.

How many km is a half marathon and a marathon?

A half marathon is 21.0975 km (13.1 miles). A marathon is 42.195 km (26.2 miles).
